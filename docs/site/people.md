# People & invites

Agora is multi-user: everyone has their own account, with an instance role
(**admin** or **member**) and per-group roles. Admins manage all of this
from the **People** button in the top bar.

## Invite someone

Two ways, both in the People pane:

- **Email invite** — enter their email and pick a role. No email is sent;
  share your Agora's URL yourself. Their account is created the first time
  they sign in with Google or Apple using that address.
- **Invite link** — create a single-use link (valid 7 days) and send it
  over any channel you like. Whoever opens it signs in and gets a member
  account.

Either way, sign-in itself is Google or Apple — see
[getting started](getting-started.md#signing-in). For the operator-side
setup (OAuth client, allowlists), see
[Sign in with Google](sign-in.md#google) or
[Sign in with Apple](sign-in.md#apple).

## Add people to a group

Membership is per group. Open any channel in the group, click **Members**,
then **Add person** — pick the account and a group role. Group admins can
add channels, manage members, and change group settings; the instance admin
can do so everywhere.

## Manage accounts

From the People pane an instance admin can promote or demote admins,
**disable** an account (kicks the person out everywhere, instantly), or
re-enable it. Anyone can delete their own account — and everything they
wrote — from **Settings → Delete account** in the iPhone app; see the
[privacy policy](privacy.html) for what that removes.
